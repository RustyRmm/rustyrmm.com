This Privacy Policy explains how RustyRMM (“we,” “us,” and “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit rustyrmm.com, sign up for or use RustyRMM Cloud, contact us, or send us feedback. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in our Terms of Use at https://rustyrmm.com/terms.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers personal information we handle for our own purposes: visitors to our website, people who sign up for a trial or subscription, account administrators and billing contacts, and people who contact us or send feedback.

When customers use the hosted Services, they direct what information is collected about their devices, users, and end clients. For that Customer Data we act as a service provider or processor on the customer’s behalf, and the customer’s own privacy notice and our agreement with the customer govern it. If you are an employee or client of one of our customers and have a question about information collected from a managed device, please contact that organization first.

If you run RustyRMM on your own infrastructure (self-hosted), the information it collects stays on your servers. We do not receive it, except as described in Section 2 for optional connections to our services.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Signup details: company name and work email address.
  • Account and billing details: administrator name and email, and billing contact details. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe; we do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Communications: the content of emails, support requests, and feedback you send, along with any contact details you include.
  • Feedback sent from the website form or from inside the product: the title and description you write, whether it is a bug report or feature request, and your email address only if you choose to include it.

Information collected automatically:

  • Terms acceptance: when you sign up, we record the version of the Terms of Use you accepted, the date and time, your IP address, and your browser’s user agent, so we can show that the agreement was made.
  • Security and abuse prevention: IP addresses are used to rate-limit signups and feedback, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a form is submitted by a person. Turnstile may process device and browser signals under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Feedback context: feedback sent from inside the product includes the RustyRMM version, your browser’s user agent, and the console page you were on, without any search terms or other query parameters.
  • Service operation: our hosting providers and network services, including Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, process technical information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the website and Services.
  • Usage for billing: for hosted instances we record the number of enabled devices, the version the instance runs, and when it was last seen, to enforce plan limits and calculate charges.

Customer Data: device inventories, telemetry, logs, script content and output, remote-session information, and similar data collected by the Services on a customer’s behalf, as described in Section 1.

Optional connections from self-hosted servers: a self-hosted RustyRMM server sends in-app feedback to our feedback service only when a user submits it, and its administrator can turn this off. Self-hosted servers and the website also download agent releases and release information from GitHub, which processes those requests under GitHub’s privacy policy.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and verify accounts, provision hosted instances, and send account emails such as confirmation links and sign-in details.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and support the Services, including rate limiting, abuse prevention, and investigating security incidents.
  • Process payments, manage subscriptions, and calculate usage-based charges.
  • Keep records of contract acceptance and comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Respond to questions, support requests, and feedback, and improve the Services based on feedback and bug reports.
  • Send service and legal notices, including notice of changes to our Terms or this policy.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use Customer Data for advertising.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Services, under obligations to protect it: Cloudflare (network, security, Turnstile, and hosting of our feedback service), Stripe (payments), GitHub (website hosting, release distribution, and tracking of submitted feedback and bug reports), and our hosting and email delivery providers.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of our customers, users, the public, or RustyRMM.
  • A successor in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to this policy’s protections.
  • Others with your direction or consent.

Feedback and bug reports are stored as issues in a private repository on GitHub that only our team can access. Text you write in feedback is stored as you wrote it, so please do not include passwords, secrets, or other sensitive information.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, which stays on your device. The Cloudflare Turnstile check on our forms may use its own technologies to tell people from automated traffic.

The RustyRMM console and the cloud signup site use cookies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in. They are not used for advertising.

6. How long we keep information

  • Unconfirmed signups are deleted when the confirmation link expires, 24 hours after signup, or when a new signup is made with the same email.
  • Account, signup, and terms-acceptance records are kept for as long as the account exists and afterward as needed to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and meet legal obligations.
  • Temporary sign-in passwords shown at setup are deleted from our records 24 hours after the instance is created.
  • Billing records are kept as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Customer Data is available for export for 30 days after a subscription ends and may then be deleted from active systems, as described in the Terms of Use. Residual backups expire through our retention cycle.
  • Feedback is kept as long as it is useful for improving the Services.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, sealed storage of instance secrets, separate databases for each hosted instance, access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact support@rustyrmm.com.

8. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our hosted Services are operated in the United States. If you access the Services from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

9. Your choices and rights

You can review and update your account details in the Services, choose whether to include your email address with feedback, and opt out of non-essential emails using the instructions in them. Service and legal notices are part of the Services and cannot be turned off while your account is active.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email legal@rustyrmm.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

For Customer Data, we will refer requests to the customer that controls the data and help that customer respond as required by our agreement with them.

10. Children

The Services are intended for businesses and professionals and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. If we make material changes, we will notify account holders by email or a prominent notice in the Services before the changes take effect.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email legal@rustyrmm.com. For account or security issues, email support@rustyrmm.com.
